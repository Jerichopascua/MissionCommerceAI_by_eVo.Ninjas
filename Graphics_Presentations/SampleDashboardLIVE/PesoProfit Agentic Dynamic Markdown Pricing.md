# PesoProfit: Agentic Dynamic Markdown Pricing

## Executive Summary

In grocery retail, markdown pricing sits directly at the critical intersection of margin protection and waste reduction:
* **Too Early:** Profit margins are unnecessarily eroded.
* **Too Late:** Unsold inventory results in 100% write-offs.

Traditional retailers manage markdowns manually using delayed data, resulting in inconsistent execution and significant missed commercial opportunities. **PesoProfit** 
introduces an autonomous, agentic pricing layer designed to eliminate manual intervention and continuously optimize markdown strategies in real time.

---

## Key Capabilities

### 1. Continuous Live Signal Ingestion
Autonomous AI agents ingest multi-dimensional store and market signals in real time:
* **Product Expiry Timelines:** Continuous tracking of remaining shelf life.
* **Inventory Positions & Rate of Sale (ROS):** Dynamic velocity tracking at SKU and store levels.
* **Competitive Intelligence:** Live price monitoring at localized store levels.

### 2. Autonomous Decisioning Engine
Unlike standard reporting dashboards, these AI agents do not merely report data—**they decide and execute autonomously**. Real-time data streams into a unified decision engine that continuously calculates and applies optimal markdown pricing throughout the trading day.

### 3. Commercial Strategy Alignment
Operational realities are dynamically balanced with core commercial goals:
* Hard and soft margin threshold enforcement
* Automated store-level waste reduction targets
* Alignment with strategic trading priorities

---

## Operational Execution & Synchronization

As inventory approaches end-of-life, price adjustments occur dynamically on a store-by-store, SKU-by-SKU, and moment-by-moment basis. 

When a price decision is made, it is pushed instantly across all operational channels:
* **Electronic Shelf Labels (ESL):** Instant in-store physical price updates.
* **E-Commerce Channels:** Unified digital storefront pricing.
* **Fulfillment & Picking Systems:** Synchronized price consistency for in-store pickers and checkout systems.

---

## Impact

* **Reduced Shrink & Waste:** Maximizes sell-through prior to expiration dates.
* **Protected Margins:** Prevents over-discounting through algorithmic precision.
* **Real-Time Agility:** Delivers retail pricing that operates seamlessly at the speed of store operations.
