HOW AI CREATES REAL-LIFE ACTORS

The LLM designs the story and structure · statistical engines draw the numbers · validators keep it realistic

① INPUTS

Requirement text · branch type & location · holiday/weather signals · past run results

② LLM SCENARIO PLANNER

vLLM on AMD, JSON-schema output: personas, missions, time windows, events, expected behaviour, metrics

③ VALIDATOR

Business constraints: stock, hours, price ranges, max uplift, no impossible baskets. Rejects & retries.

④ BEHAVIOR ENGINE

Samples counts, times, baskets from seeded distributions: deterministic & replayable

⑤ RUN

Actors operate PesoWeb → score
