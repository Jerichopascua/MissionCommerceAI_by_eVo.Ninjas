# 01 — Event Overview

## Summary

- **Name:** AMD Developer Hackathon: ACT III (AMD × lablab.ai)
- **Theme:** Build AI agents and high-performance AI applications on AMD GPUs in the cloud
- **Format:** Hybrid
  - **Online build:** 12–18 October 2026
  - **On-site phase (Italy):** 17–18 October 2026 — Rome, Milan, Imperia
- **Prize pool:** $12,000+
- **Perk:** Free $100 AMD credits for new AMD AI Developer Program members

- [ ] Register on **lablab.ai** (Enroll / "Sign up with AMD")

- [ ] Read: Hackathon Guidelines, Getting Started Guide, lablab.ai Hackathon Rule Book

## Team rules

- Team size: **1–6 people**
- Find teammates via the lablab dashboard or Discord
- Open to all experience levels

## General terms (from page)

- Submissions must be **original and MIT-compliant**
- Prize distribution may take up to **90 days**
- Rules, prizes, terms may change at organizers' discretion
- All development runs in the cloud — no local hardware needed
