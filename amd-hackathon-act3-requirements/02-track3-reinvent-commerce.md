# 02 — Track 3: Reinvent Commerce  ⭐ OUR TRACK

**Track partner:** Vibe Generation
**Tagline:** Help a business understand its customers and take better action.

## Problem statement (from brief)

Retailers have product data, transactions, inventory, reviews, support conversations, and marketing results. These sources are often **disconnected**, which makes it difficult to turn them into useful decisions.

**Goal:** Build a product that helps a business **attract, serve, convert, or retain** customers.

## Idea areas (suggested by organizers)

- Shopping assistants
- Product discovery and recommendations
- Customer support
- Demand and inventory planning
- Product catalogue enrichment
- Review and sentiment analysis
- Promotion planning
- Churn prevention
- Accessible and multilingual shopping
- In-store computer vision

## Demo requirements

- Show how the product turns **customer or business data** into a **useful recommendation or action**.
- If the product **predicts demand, sales, or customer behaviour**:
  - Explain **how the prediction was calculated**.
  - **Do NOT use a language model as the only forecasting method.** (Need a real statistical/ML model — e.g., time-series, gradient boosting — with the LLM as an explainer/orchestrator at most.)

## "Good projects will show"

- [ ] A **real customer or merchant problem**
- [ ] A **useful commercial action**
- [ ] **Relevant recommendations or personalization**
- [ ] A **measurable effect** on revenue, margin, conversion, retention, inventory, or service quality

## General challenge rules (apply to all tracks)

- Solve a real business problem for a real user.
- May use LLMs, computer vision, audio, agents, predictive models, or a combination.
- **The product must work**, and **AMD technology must power a meaningful part of it**.
