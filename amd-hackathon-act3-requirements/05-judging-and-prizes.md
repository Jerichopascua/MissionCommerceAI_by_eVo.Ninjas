# 05 — Judging Criteria & Prizes

## Main judging criteria (all tracks)

| Criterion | What judges look for |
|---|---|
| **Application of Technology** | How effectively the chosen model(s) are integrated into the solution |
| **Presentation** | Clarity and effectiveness of the project presentation |
| **Business Value** | Impact and practical value; fit to business areas |
| **Originality** | Uniqueness and creativity; novel approaches and demonstrated behaviors |
