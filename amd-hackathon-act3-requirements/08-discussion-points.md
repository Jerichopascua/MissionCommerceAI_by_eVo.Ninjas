# 08 — Discussion Points for Team Kickoff

## Decisions to make

1. **Problem & user** — which merchant/customer pain point? (pick from Track 3 idea list or our own)
2. **Data source** — public retail dataset vs. synthetic vs. real merchant data (privacy!)
3. **AMD workload** — what runs on AMD? (LLM via vLLM/ROCm, forecasting model, recsys, vision)
4. **Forecasting approach** — non-LLM model (e.g., Prophet / LightGBM / PyTorch) + LLM explanation layer
5. **Measurable KPI** — which metric do we move and how do we prove it in the demo?
6. **Partner prizes** — go for Evolus (needs ≥2 building blocks, containerized app)? Google (details TBC)?
7. **Stack** — backend (.NET / Python), frontend, hosting for the app URL
8. **Roles** — who owns model, backend, UI, demo video, deck

## Questions to confirm at kickoff / Discord

- Google prize eligibility and required Google technologies
- Amount of AMD Developer Cloud credits/GPU hours per team
- Any restrictions on datasets
- Demo video length/format limits
