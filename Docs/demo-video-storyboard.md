# Demo video storyboard (about 75 seconds)

Style reference: the "eGov Agent" product video. It uses a clean app on screen, short on-screen captions, one idea per scene, a live step list that ticks while the AI works, and a title card at the start and end.

Rule: **rehearse and storyboard on `ui/prototype`; record the final video from the real running system** with real run numbers. The prototype shows illustrative data and says so in its header.

| # | Time | On screen | Caption | Voiceover idea |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 0:00-0:04 | Title card, dark theme: "Fast-forward a retail world." | See how AI responds. | Retail systems fail where test data never looks. |
| 1 | 0:04-0:12 | Control Center. Owner sign-ups stream in. One owner is downsized by their plan tier. | Owners sign up. The world builds itself. | Simulated owners register on the real PesoWeb and open branches. |
| 2 | 0:12-0:24 | Day-to-Day Live. Clock runs, parents and seniors in the morning, a student at lunch. | Real customers, real habits. | Each customer has a personality and a routine. |
| 3 | 0:24-0:32 | An injected incident appears at 11:40 (milk 50 vs 42). KPI tile "Exceptions open: 1, not yet caught". | We cause a problem on purpose. | The simulator knows the truth. Does the system notice? |
| 4 | 0:32-0:44 | Caught vs Missed scorecard. Click the cash variance. Investigator steps tick: pull shift, read cash movements, check adjustments, correlate, explain. | The AI investigates. | Unexplained variance, with evidence, not an accusation. |
| 5 | 0:44-0:54 | Missions and Growth. Mission strips, then Week 0 vs Week 3 table. | Detect the mission. Take the action. | Same seed, with and without the AI's actions. |

## Recording tips
- 1920x1080 browser window, zoom 100%, hide bookmarks.
- Use `#live` at the 1x speed for scene 2; rehearse the order once so the incident lands during the voiceover.
- Scene 4: open the drawer by clicking the first incident row; the steps take about 4 seconds.
- For the real video, replace each prototype screen with the matching real screen. The captions can stay.
- Keep the "illustrative data" badge in any prototype clip. Do not present prototype figures as results.
