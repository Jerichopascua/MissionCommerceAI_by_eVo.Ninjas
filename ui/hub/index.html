Challenges retailers face today

Thin margins leave little room for guesswork, and the day's problems are usually found after the day is over.

Waste

Fresh stock reaches its date before it sells, and a markdown that comes too late or too deep costs more than it saves.

Pricing

Prices are set by habit. Nobody knows which items could carry a few more pesos, or which sell below what they cost to stock.

Benchmarking

It is hard to tell whether a branch is doing well or badly compared with the others, until the month-end report.

Anomalies

Cash differences, unrecorded deliveries and expired stock are found late, and nobody is sure whose job it is.

The Intelligence Hub

Built into PesoWeb, so the numbers come from the same system that rings up the sales. Every screen below is the real screen.

Sales, gross margin, stock turnover, days of stock, shrinkage, expiry at risk and open exceptions, side by side by branch and by region, for the period and category you choose, against each branch's sales and waste targets. It refreshes itself every minute.
My tasks lists what is waiting for the roles you hold: AI price proposals to decide, alerts from your rules, and reorders to place. Urgent first, each with the button or link that settles it.
Eight plain rules (expired stock, cash differences, selling below cost, shrinkage, too much stock and more), plus AI Monitoring, which compares each branch with its own recent past and flags the unusual: a day when sales halved, a cash difference far above normal, a burst of returns. You set the limit, the role or the named person, and whether to email them. PesoWeb checks them itself every few minutes, and can also push alerts and price changes to another system by webhook.
AI Replenish checks whether each product will last until a delivery arrives, given how fast it sells, and suggests how much to order and why. Perishables are capped at what sells before they expire. Tick the ones you want and PesoWeb drafts Pending purchase orders with the usual supplier; stock changes only when you receive them.
AI Customer Mission reads baskets (the hour, the number of items, the value, fresh food or not) and says how many were quick top-ups, dinner runs, weekly restocks or late-night stops, and what that means for stock and staffing. It is a hint: it cannot see intent.
Every AI price proposal arrives with its reason, the margin before and after, what the AI learned from this shop's own sales, a cautious profit estimate, what rivals charge, and the rule checks. Approve, reject, or approve at a different price.
AI Impact compares equal stretches of days before and after the AI started changing prices, and AI branches against a branch you keep with the AI off as a control. It shows a difference, not proof, and says so on the page.
Switch each AI feature on or off, set per branch whether the AI may mark prices down and from what hour, its region and its targets, and (for the head company) see every company's AI settings in one table. PesoWeb enforces all of it.

How it works

PesoWeb enforces the rules

Margin floors, the deepest markdown, how often a price may change, who may approve what, and the on and off switches. The AI cannot override them.

The AI proposes

It reads costs, stock, expiry and sales, then proposes markdowns and price changes through PesoWeb's own API, with its reasoning and a prediction it will be scored on.

A person decides

Proposals wait in one Approval Center with the evidence. Everything is logged, and a change can be undone.

What it does not do yet

Said plainly
  • The shoppers and their reaction to price are simulated; the catalog, PesoWeb and the AI's decisions are real. The rival prices in the demo are a simulated feed.
  • On thin real margins the benefit is a few percent of gross margin, not a transformation. The larger waste reduction is on generated shops with normal margins.
  • AI Customer Mission is a hint: on simulated shoppers it matched their real mission on about 6 baskets in 10.
  • Alerts email only if the company has set up its mail server; the webhook is generic, not a connector for one product; desktop alerts work while the page is open. SMS and mobile push are not built.
  • AI Replenish and its draft purchase orders do not send anything to the supplier; a person does. Its demo scenario is staged.
  • Not yet tested on the AMD GPU; that comes last.
